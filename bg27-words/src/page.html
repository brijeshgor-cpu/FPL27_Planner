Modes

Daily is a five-letter word in British English. Guesses must be real words.

Football, Movies, Geography and Brands are 4 to 7 letters, shown by the grid. The word is a club, player, film, place, brand or a term from that world. Any letters are allowed as guesses, because names aren't in the dictionary.

The clock starts when you type your first letter. Finish, add a nickname and your result joins that day's board. Fewest guesses wins; time breaks ties.
