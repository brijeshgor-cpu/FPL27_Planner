A five-letter word in British English. Guesses must be real words. The clock starts at your first letter, and your result goes on the leaderboard.

Categories

Football, Movies, Geography and Brands. Words are 4 to 7 letters, shown by the grid. Any letters are allowed, because names aren't in the dictionary. Stuck? Tap Hint to see what kind of word it is, such as "Football player". There's no clock and no leaderboard, so take your time.
