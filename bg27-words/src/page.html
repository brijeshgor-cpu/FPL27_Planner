Daily

A five-letter word in British English. Guesses must be real words. The clock starts at your first letter, and your result goes on the leaderboard.

Categories

Football, Movies, Geography and Brands. Words are 4 to 7 letters, shown by the grid. Any letters are allowed, because names aren't in the dictionary. Each word comes with a gentle hint, such as "A famous name". Once the game ends, you see exactly what kind of word it was. There's no clock and no leaderboard, so take your time (Kids zone is the exception).

Kids zone

Easy, fun words: Disney and Pixar, animals, countries, magical words, arts and crafts, fashion, school, space, hobbies and food. Words are 4 to 7 letters and the hint is always on. It has its own leaderboard with the same points as Daily, so the clock runs from the first letter.

Daily leaderboard

Points: 1 guess 600, 2 guesses 500, 3 guesses 400, 4 guesses 300, 5 guesses 200, 6 guesses 100, a miss 0. Each day ranks by fewest guesses, then fastest time, and identical results share a place. Weekly and Monthly add up your points. All-time shows the ten best single games.

Your stats and devices

There's no login. The first device to post with a nickname owns it, so nobody else can post as you. Tap Stats to see your global rank, win rate, streaks and points. To play on another device, open Stats, tap Get a link code and enter the code on the new device. Tap any name on the leaderboard to see their stats.
